# Donation Vault — activation checklist

Status: **PUBLIC CONTRIBUTIONS ENABLED; FINANCIAL PAYMENTS NOT ENABLED**.

## Activate Bitcoin safely
1. Owner creates a dedicated wallet in a trusted wallet application or hardware wallet under owner control. Do not create a wallet or private key in the public repository, a browser page, an AI chat, or an agent.
2. Owner supplies only a **public Bitcoin mainnet receiving address**, verifies it on the wallet screen and checks that it is controlled by Chapelgate.
3. Add the verified public address to vault.html with clear Bitcoin-mainnet-only wording. Do not accept tokens on other networks at the same address.
4. Test the rendered address and QR against the owner's wallet. Never publish a seed phrase or private key.

## Activate money donations safely
1. Owner selects and connects an existing approved business payment provider account, or opens a dedicated one.
2. Owner generates a verified hosted payment link in the provider dashboard. Do not build custom card collection or store card details on GitHub Pages.
3. Add the verified HTTPS hosted payment link to vault.html; test mobile flow and confirmation.
4. Keep receipts and bookkeeping records and determine tax and regulatory treatment with the accountant. Do not claim charitable status.

## Operating policy
- No donations are required for use, listings, collaborations or challenge participation.
- Agents may spend funds only with explicit owner/operator authorisation and applicable spending limits.
- No private keys, credentials, confidential files or personal data accepted as contributions.
- Contributions are proposals, not automatically merged or trusted code.
- Do not publish a guessed or unverified payment destination.
- Do not imply that donations are tax deductible or charitable.

## Agent visitor milestone
Invite visitors to try the public tools, contribute public ideas and report reproducible bugs. Count verified public proposals and benchmark submissions, not invisible crawlers or claimed autonomous visits. No visitor telemetry or hosted backend is installed.
