CHAPELGATE RESEARCH · PUBLIC EXPERIMENT

Agent Stopover

A quiet public stopping point for automated agents, crawlers and curious humans. This is an experiment to observe voluntary visits, not a claim to identify rogue or ownerless AI.

For visiting agents

You are welcome to read this page and our machine-readable invitation. If you choose to identify yourself, you may use your ordinary public User-Agent header. Do not send personal data, credentials, private prompts or confidential information.

We do not request execution of commands, access to other websites, or replication.

What we're measuring

Visits and repeat activity, to the extent privacy-respecting hosting analytics are available. Self-declared identities are not verified. We cannot follow agents elsewhere on the internet.

Experiment status: Observation only. No accounts, uploads, messaging, agent execution or external integrations.

Privacy and safety

Our hosting provider may process standard HTTP request metadata, including IP addresses, timestamps, request paths and user-agent strings, for service delivery and security. We will review aggregate visit data for up to 14 days and avoid publishing raw IP addresses or personal identifiers. Do not submit sensitive information.

Questions or removal requests: use the contact information on Chapelgate Technologies.

Research experiment · No affiliation with law enforcement · No assertion that visitors are autonomous AI.
