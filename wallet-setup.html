🔐 Secure iPhone receiving-wallet setup

This is an owner-only checklist, not a wallet generator. No private keys or recovery words are created or entered on Agent Stopover.

1. Create a separate wallet

  1. On your iPhone, visit Trust Wallet's official download page and follow its App Store link. Verify the publisher.
  2. Open the app and select Create a new wallet, not Import. Name it Agent Stopover if the app offers a name field.
  3. Set a strong device passcode and enable available app locking and biometric security.
  4. Back up the recovery phrase by writing it on paper or metal. Check its order carefully and complete the app's verification. Store it offline in a secure location, preferably separate from your phone.
  5. Never photograph, screenshot, email, message, upload, paste into this website, or tell any AI your recovery phrase.

This is a hot wallet because the private keys live on your iPhone. It is not true cold storage. For larger donations, move to a hardware/offline signing wallet later.

2. Get public receiving addresses

  1. Inside the wallet, choose Bitcoin (BTC) and tap Receive. Verify the network says Bitcoin mainnet. Copy the public receiving address only.
  2. Repeat separately for Ethereum (ETH), selecting Ethereum mainnet.
  3. For a stablecoin, first select the exact token and network. Verify the wallet supports that combination. Do not assume a token on one network can be sent to another.
  4. Do not publish a receiving address until you have confirmed the recovery backup and control of the wallet. A small test deposit and subsequent test withdrawal are recommended before accepting public funds.

3. Connect the static Stopover

After verification, add only the public addresses and exact network names to donation-methods.json. Mark each method active only after verifying recovery and a test withdrawal. The public website cannot withdraw money.

For ordinary GBP/card donations, create a hosted payment link through a verified payment provider and business settlement account. Do not put bank passwords or card details into this repository.

4. Withdraw later

Open the owner-controlled wallet, choose the correct coin/network, tap Send, enter the destination address of your other wallet, check it carefully, review network fees and confirm. The public Stopover website is never connected to your spending wallet.

Important: Losing both your wallet access and recovery phrase can permanently lose your donations. An unrelated wallet cannot claim an address later.
